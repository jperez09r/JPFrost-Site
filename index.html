— 01 / The Book

An elevator pitch, for the patient reader.

Logline

At the edge of an empire that catalogues every wielder it can find, a winter province has learned to hide its own. The shards inherited there are kept dormant; the bloodlines are kept quiet; the children are taught a third law before they are taught the first two — don't be remembered. But the empire is patient, and a single anomaly at a checkpoint is enough to turn a name into a number. Crystalus Beta is a novel about what survives when concealment fails — the quiet resistance doing the slow work of catching the ones the cataloguers find first, the inheritance that arrives whether you ask for it or not, and the older question waiting underneath: whether what an empire wants to use and what it wants to erase were ever different things.

— 02 / The Author

JP Frost, in his own words.

Self-portrait · illustrated

JP Frost writes under a pen name and keeps the rest deliberately vague. By day he works as an application engineer — mapping problems, architecting solutions, building systems that have to actually hold under load — and brings the same instincts to fiction: structure first, payoff earned, every piece doing real work. He writes in fragments wherever he happens to be, on transit and between meetings and in margins, and lets the longer hours of the week shape those fragments into something with a spine. Before the novel there were years of poems, and before the poems there was a story he tried to make into a game in middle school. Crystalus Beta is the version of that story he finally had the patience to tell.

He is drawn to stories with a long horizon — worlds large enough to disappear into, faction politics with no easy sides, characters whose growth is measured in seasons rather than scenes. He grew up on epics from every medium that took their worldbuilding seriously, and on serialized storytelling that trusted its audience to wait. He cares about structure: a setup that earns its turn, a turn that earns its cost, a cost that earns the next book. He wants to write the kind of story he would line up to read.

He is currently unrepresented, and is open to query from literary agents working in adult fantasy.

A note before the book

This is a pre-publication site for a debut novel that is finished. There are no preorder links, no merchandise, no newsletter waiting to catch your email on the way out. What there is: a book — Crystalus Beta: The Pale Lycrux Legend — a standalone adult literary fantasy with room to grow into a series, and an open invitation to literary agents working in the space.

I'll post here when there's something real to say — a note on craft, a turn in the work, a thing worth marking. Not often, and never for the sake of it. If you're an agent, the contact page has what you need. If you're a reader who wandered in early: welcome. The door was left open on purpose.
